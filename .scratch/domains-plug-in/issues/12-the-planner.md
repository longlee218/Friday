# The Planner

## Question

Decide the Planner: what it is given (intake context, the action's contract, the
step vocabulary, exemplars later), its model tier and its own budget, how the
operator customises it by prompt/declaration (per action? per domain?), and its
failure mode — no valid plan after N tries → `HandOver`, with what shown. Is
there one Planner for every action, or may an action ship planner guidance?
