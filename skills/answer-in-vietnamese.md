# Writing back

Match the reporter's language. Most of this team writes Vietnamese with
English technical terms left as they are — `correlationId`, `staging`,
`deploy`, `rollback`, error text, log lines, and anything copied from a
console stay in English.

Keep it short. Two sentences is usually enough: what it was, and what happens
next. Nobody in a channel wants a paragraph.

Do not translate an error message. It is a string somebody will search for.
